1 Information We Collect

The App does not require account registration and does not ask users to provide personal information such as name, phone number, address, passport data, or payment information.

The App may store or process the following types of information:

  • App settings and preferences;
  • Selected language;
  • Selected vehicle category;
  • Learning progress;
  • Favorite questions;
  • Mistakes and statistics;
  • App usage data within the App;
  • Advertising identifiers;
  • Device and technical information;
  • Diagnostic information related to app performance and advertising.

The App itself does not determine, store, or request the user’s country. The App does not use GPS, location permissions, IP geolocation services, or a developer-owned backend to determine the user’s location.

Some data is stored locally on your device and is not sent to our own servers.

2 Local Data Storage

The App may store learning progress, mistakes, favorites, statistics, settings, selected language, selected vehicle category, and other app preferences locally on your device.

This information is used to make the App work correctly and to improve your learning experience.

You can delete this data by clearing the App data or uninstalling the App from your device.

3 Advertising

The App may display advertisements through third-party advertising services.

The App may use the following third-party advertising services:

  • Google AdMob
  • Yandex Mobile Ads

The App first attempts to load ads through Google AdMob. If an ad is not available, the App may attempt to load an ad through Yandex Mobile Ads. If no ad is available, no advertisement is shown.

Third-party advertising services may collect and process certain data, including advertising identifiers, device information, app interaction data, approximate location inferred by the advertising provider, IP address, and diagnostic information.

These services process data according to their own privacy policies.

4 Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services for advertising, app functionality, diagnostics, or technical purposes.

Third-party services used by the App may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Yandex Mobile Ads

The App no longer uses any third-party IP geolocation service to determine the user’s country.

Third-party services may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

5 Analytics and Diagnostics

The App may process technical and diagnostic information to understand how the App works, detect errors, improve performance, and maintain stability.

This may include information such as device type, operating system version, app version, crash logs, performance data, advertising performance data, and app interaction data.

6 Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for general users who want to study road traffic rules and driving test questions.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

7 Data Sharing

We do not sell users’ personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services used in the App, such as advertising or technical service providers. These providers process data according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

8 Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

9 Your Choices

You may limit or reset advertising identifiers through your device settings.

You may delete locally stored App data by clearing the App data or uninstalling the App.

You may also manage advertising personalization settings through your device settings or through privacy controls provided by advertising platforms.
