1. Controller and scope

This Policy explains how the independent developer operating under the MontePixel Studio name handles information in connection with the Test Libreta de Conducir Uruguay mobile application (the “App”) for Android.

The App is an independent educational tool for practicing questions, mock exams, and road signs. It is not an official app of the Government of Uruguay or any departmental government.

2. Information you provide

The App requires no account or sign-in and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or other contact details. If you contact the developer voluntarily by email or Telegram, the message is used only to respond, provide support, protect the App, or comply with law, and is kept only as long as reasonably necessary.

3. Information stored locally

The App may store on your device the selected language and theme, identifiers of favorite or incorrectly answered questions and signs, total time spent, and aggregate counts of views, answers, mistakes, and completed, passed, or failed exams.

This supports favorites, mistake review, statistics, and achievements. It is not sent to the developer or used to identify you. Device backups may include it depending on Google and your settings.

4. Advertising and device information

The App may show ads supplied by Google AdMob and Yandex Mobile Ads. These providers and authorized partners may process AAID and other identifiers; IP address and inferred approximate location; device, system, language, time zone, screen, network, and connection data; ad requests, impressions, clicks, frequency; and security, fraud, performance, or diagnostic data.

They may use this data for contextual or permitted personalized ads, frequency limits, measurement, reports, security, and fraud prevention. The developer may receive aggregated reports, not your name, email, or phone. Depending on region, age, settings, and law, the App may request consent or offer privacy choices.

5. Sharing and sale

The developer does not sell or rent personal information. Google, Yandex, and their authorized advertising partners may process data for the purposes above. Information may also be disclosed to comply with a valid legal obligation, enforce terms, or protect users, the App, or others.

6. External links, government sources and platforms

The App provides links to official government information and public documents from the Intendencia de Montevideo and Intendencia de Canelones.

Government information sources linked by the App:

  • Guía Nacional de Conducción – Autos y Motos — Intendencia de Montevideo: official PDF
  • Guía para Conductores Profesionales — Intendencia de Montevideo: official PDF
  • Señales de Tránsito — Intendencia de Montevideo: official PDF
  • Señales de Tránsito — Intendencia de Canelones: official PDF

The App is an independent educational application and does not represent, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by, any government entity.

Government document links and the developer’s Telegram channel open outside the App. Those services may receive ordinary technical data under their policies; the developer does not control that processing or receive your browsing activity. Google Play separately processes download, installation, purchase, or diagnostic data under its rules.

7. Permissions and device access

Apart from advertising-related identifiers when enabled and permitted, the App does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or personal files. External links open only after you select them.

8. Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you reset it in the App, clear App data, or uninstall it. Advertising providers retain data under their own policies. You can manage consent in available privacy controls and device settings. Support-correspondence requests may be sent to the developer.

9. Security

The developer minimizes data and keeps educational progress locally. Advertising providers protect the information they process. No electronic method is completely secure, but reasonable measures are applied to the limited information under the developer’s control.

10. Children

The App is not specifically directed to children and the developer does not knowingly request their personal information. Advertising and age settings must follow region, age, store classification, and law. Contact the developer if a child sent information through an external channel.

11. International processing

Advertising providers and external services may process information outside your country. Their policies describe relevant safeguards and legal bases.

12. Changes to this policy

The developer may update this Policy when features, advertising, data practices, or requirements change. The current version shows its last-updated date; material changes will be published with the applicable App version.
